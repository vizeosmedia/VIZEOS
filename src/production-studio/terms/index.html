Terms of service

These terms describe Production Studio's optional connection to YouTube.

Last updated September 24, 2026

Using the connection

Production Studio is a Vizeos Media desktop production app. An authorized channel owner may connect a YouTube channel to a workspace through Google's sign-in flow. The owner chooses the channel and may disconnect it in the app's Settings.

Private uploads and review

When automatic upload is enabled, newly completed and verified full video exports from that workspace are sent to the selected channel as private videos. The app does not automatically make videos public. The channel owner remains responsible for reviewing each upload and deciding whether, when, and how to publish it.

Content and third-party terms

The channel owner is responsible for having the rights needed to upload its videos, music, and other content, and for complying with YouTube's policies and Terms of Service. Google sign-in and YouTube delivery are also subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

Privacy and questions

Our Production Studio privacy policy explains the Google data used by this connection and how to disconnect or revoke access. Contact hello@vizeosmedia.com with questions.
