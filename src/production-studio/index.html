Production Studio

Our desktop production app helps our team prepare, verify, and deliver videos for the YouTube channels we operate. Each channel is connected separately by its owner.

Private YouTube delivery

After the owner connects a channel, Production Studio can upload a newly completed and verified full video export to that channel as private. The owner can review the result in YouTube Studio. Connecting a channel does not upload older exports, and the app does not make videos public.

What access is requested?

Google sign-in asks for YouTube upload access and read-only access to identify the selected channel. Production Studio uses that access only for the connected workspace's private delivery and channel checks. The owner can disconnect in the app's Settings.

Questions about access or data?

Read our Production Studio privacy policy for the data we use and how to disconnect or revoke access. You can contact hello@vizeosmedia.com.
